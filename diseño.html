02 / OVERVIEW

From cultural identity to a functioning fashion business.

SPACE JOB began when I looked back at my roots and found the strongest source of inspiration in Cuba. The brand combines contemporary streetwear with color, energy and references to the culture that shaped me.

The project evolved beyond apparel design into a complete digital product: a visual identity, campaign system, online storefront, checkout experience, payment integration and customer communication flow.
