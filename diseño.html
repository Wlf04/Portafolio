02 / STORY

From cultural identity to a functioning fashion business.

SPACE JOB began when I looked toward my roots and found inspiration in Cuba — its energy, color and identity.

What started as apparel design grew into a complete brand system: visual identity, campaign direction, an online storefront, payments and the customer purchase experience.

PROJECT SCOPE

I created the visual identity, apparel graphics and digital experience, then developed the storefront and integrated the commerce flow.

03 / ORIGIN

Rooted in Cuba.

“I wanted the clothing to carry identity, color and history — not just graphics.”

04 / IDENTITY

A visual system that works on fabric and on screen.

The identity uses a compact symbol, strong typography and flexible graphic treatments that can move between garments, campaign imagery and the online store.
