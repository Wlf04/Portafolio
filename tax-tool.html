01 / THE IDEA

One workspace, fewer disconnected steps.

Tax preparation often involves collecting client information, completing forms and organizing documents across multiple steps. Tax Tool brings those activities together in a single guided interface.

The goal is straightforward: make the intake process easier to navigate, reduce repetitive manual work and keep the information needed for each client organized from start to finish.
