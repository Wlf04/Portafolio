01 / THE IDEA

Make studying feel simpler.

Preparing for a cybersecurity certification involves returning to concepts, building confidence and staying consistent. FARO was created as a focused tool for that process, without unnecessary complexity.

The idea: a practical piece of software dedicated to Security+ study, with a small scope and a complete, usable implementation.

02 / CURRENT BUILD

Simple in scope. Working in practice.

FARO is already functional. Instead of presenting it as a large platform, this project demonstrates a focused product idea brought to a working state.

LIVE / FUNCTIONAL BUILD

A published study experience with 500 bilingual questions for the Security+ SY0-701 exam.
